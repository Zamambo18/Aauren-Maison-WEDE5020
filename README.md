# Aauren Maison - Luxury Fashion Boutique

**Location:** Oceans Mall, uMhlanga, Durban, South Africa

**Project Overview:** Aauren Maison is a high-fashion luxury boutique targeting exclusive clientele at Oceans Mall. This website showcases the brand's identity, services, and location.

**Website Goals:**
- Establish luxury brand presence online
- Showcase services (Personal Styling, Bespoke Tailoring)
- Drive enquiries and store visits at Oceans Mall

**Key Features:**
- Responsive 5-page website (Home, About, Services, Enquiry, Contact)
- Luxury black and gold design aesthetic
- Contact form and business information

**Project Structure:**
- HTML5, CSS3
- Organized folders: css/, images/
